Stable isotope data reduction

isoharness turns the raw output of an isotope ratio mass spectrometer into calibrated δ values, using the reference materials measured in the same run. Each correction is a separate step that shows its numbers, so you can see what it does and change it. It is meant for students learning how the corrections work and for researchers processing their own runs.

Instruments

  • EA-IRMS

    Available

    An elemental analyzer coupled to an isotope ratio mass spectrometer, for δ¹⁵N and δ¹³C of solid samples, with %N, %C, and C:N. Reads Isodat exports.

    Open

  • GasBench N₂O

    Planned

    δ¹⁵N and δ¹⁸O of nitrate by the denitrifier method, and δ¹⁵N of organic nitrogen after persulfate oxidation, both measured as N₂O.

  • GasBench carbonate

    Planned

    δ¹³C and δ¹⁸O of carbonate, measured on the CO₂ released by phosphoric acid.
